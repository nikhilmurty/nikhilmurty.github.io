Plant Watering Alert System

For the forgetful plant parent

Background

I love having plants around the house, but I am terrible at remembering to water them. My problem is that some plants need to be watered more frequently than others, and I always forget how often to water each plant. So I decided to build a system that would tell me when my plants need watering.
